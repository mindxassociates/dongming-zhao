# Pages CMS editor guide

## English article

1. Open Pages CMS and choose **English blog / 英文博客**.
2. Create a new item.
3. Enter the title, a permanent lowercase URL slug, summary, date, and article body.
4. Leave **Keep private as draft** on while reviewing.
5. Add a featured image only when useful, and describe it in **Image description**.
6. Turn the draft setting off and save when the article is ready to publish.

## 中文文章

1. 打开 **中文博客 / Chinese blog**。
2. 新建文章，填写标题、网址短名、摘要、日期和正文。
3. 审阅期间保留“草稿”开关；正式发布时关闭并保存。
4. 如果上传封面图，请同时填写准确的图片说明。

## Connecting translations

English and Chinese articles are independent. If two articles are translations of each other, give both the exact same **Translation key / 双语对应标识**. The language link appears only when both versions are published.

**Save** commits the change to GitHub. A non-draft post becomes public after Cloudflare finishes the automatic build. Never upload client or health information.
